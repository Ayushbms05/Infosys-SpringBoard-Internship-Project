OUR MISSION

Why Literacy Matters

Over 250 million adults in India face daily obstacles due to functional illiteracy. AksharGyan bridges this gap with accessible, multi-sensory AI learning designed specifically for adult and first-generation learners.

Empowering Daily Independence

Gain confidence to read bus route signs, banking deposit slips, medical prescriptions, and smartphone messages without relying on others.

AI-Personalized Learning Pace

Instant speech feedback and adaptive exercises adjust dynamically to your progress, ensuring zero pressure and maximum retention.

Native Language First

Learn functional literacy directly in your mother tongue, seamlessly building bridges to English and regional state languages.

3-STEP LEARNING JOURNEY

How AksharGyan Works

A clear, proven roadmap tailored for adult learners. Build practical reading, writing, and speaking skills in just 5 to 10 minutes a day.

STEP 01

Initial AI Diagnostic

Start with a gentle, 5-minute visual & voice evaluation to identify what you already know. No pass/fail anxiety — just finding your comfortable starting baseline.

Adaptive Baseline
STEP 02

Multi-Sensory Practice

Learn letters and words through sight, sound, speech evaluation, and touch handwriting tracing on screen. Instant audio feedback guides every syllable.

Voice + Touch Tracing
STEP 03

Real-World Independence

Apply your skills immediately to read bus destination boards, medicine packets, bank slips, and mobile OTPs. Build lasting confidence for daily life.

Practical Fluency

REAL-WORLD CURRICULUM

Skills for Everyday Life

We don't teach abstract grammar rules. We teach practical literacy modules you encounter every day in your community.

🏦
Essential

Banking & Money

Read deposit slips, understand ATM screen buttons, verify passbook balance entries, and handle UPI scan payments with confidence.

  • ATM Cash Withdrawal Prompts
  • Cheque & Deposit Slip Forms
  • Balance SMS & OTP Verification
🚌
Mobility

Transit & Wayfinding

Navigate state bus route nameboards, railway platform indicators, metro tokens, and regional highway directional signs.

  • Bus Destination Boards
  • Train Platform & Coach Numbers
  • Street Signs & Landmark Maps
💊
Vital

Health & Medicine

Understand doctor prescription dosage timings (morning/night), expiry dates on medicine strips, and clinic appointment slips.

  • Medicine Strip Expiry & Dosages
  • Hospital Room & Ward Signs
  • Emergency Contact Numbers
📱
Digital

Phone & Utilities

Read essential mobile SMS alerts, recharge validity reminders, electricity/water bill due dates, and voice-assisted typing.

  • Reading 6-Digit OTP Messages
  • Electricity & Water Bill Receipts
  • Smartphone Keyboard Typing
⚖️
Civic

Civic Rights & Forms

Fill basic application forms, understand ration card allocations, verify government scheme benefits, and sign your own name.

  • Signature & Identity Recognition
  • Ration Shop Quota Records
  • Basic Application Form Entries
🛒
Commerce

Workplace & Trade

Read wholesale price tags, customer bills, package weight labels, and inventory lists for small shops and daily commerce.

  • Price & Weight Item Labels
  • Daily Khata (Ledger) Entries
  • Customer Purchase Receipts
